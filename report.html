Keep match fraction as a diagnostic

Current evidence supports recording the metadata without adding another selection. No-cut removes zero additional objects or area. A 98% threshold duplicates existing cuts in this sample; a 99% threshold removes 11,002 selected galaxies without a measured benefit. This is a decision for the current evidence, not a guarantee that future low-match tiles are harmless.

Limits and revisit condition

All observed historical guard rejections are published and included; none remains absent from the measured low tail. The ordinary count-available controls and the 27 geographically clustered current manifests are not a representative survey sample. This investigation cannot prove future low-match tiles safe or rule out multiplicative shear bias. Revisit the no-cut decision when low-match tiles retain selected galaxies, their quality changes coherently, or the selection changes enough to admit these sparse fields.
