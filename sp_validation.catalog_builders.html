sp_validation.catalog_builders¶

CATALOG BUILDERS.

Catalogue construction pipeline — the runner classes that create, mask, and calibrate joint comprehensive catalogues, plus the run_* entry-point functions. Built on the catalogue data layer in catalog (imported here as sp_cat), which supplies the read/write/column-access/matching primitives.

Author:

Martin Kilbinger
